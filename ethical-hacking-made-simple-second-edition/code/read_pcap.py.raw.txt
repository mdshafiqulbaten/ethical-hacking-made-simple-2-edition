     carries the HTTP request, packet 6 the status line, packet 8 the
     page. Packets 10 to 12 close the connection (FA means FIN and ACK).

     the page travel in plain text: anyone who can see the network can
     read them. That is why Chapter 9 matters.

 QUICK CHALLENGE

 A scan reports port 8080 as filtered. A colleague says "so nothing is
 running there." Is that safe to conclude?

 Suggested answer: No. Filtered means the scanner got no useful an‐
 swer, often because a firewall dropped the packets. A service may be
 running behind the firewall. The honest statement is "I could not
 tell," followed by a plan to check from another viewpoint, such as the
 server itself.

Key Takeaways
 A subnet and a scope card are the same idea: a range you may
 touch. Check membership with code, not by eye.
 An address finds a machine; a port finds a program. A service is a
 program listening on a port.
 Open, closed, and filtered are three different answers to one
 question.
 A request and its reply cross the network in plain text unless
 something encrypts them.
